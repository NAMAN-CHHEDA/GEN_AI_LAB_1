The "MiFID" part usually penalises outputs that copy training images, so check the Evaluation page for how it works and avoid memorising.

5. Who is scored on what

  • Individual (15): your model builds, metrics, failure analysis, hardware disclosure, and viva defence.
  • Team (15): repo quality, combined comparison tables, joint analysis, reproducibility.
  • Viva (5, individual): depth of understanding of your own implementation, justifying decisions, answering "what if" questions. The PDF says leaderboard rank is not what's judged there.
The PDF says you can't use Claude or a similar assistant for core architecture decisions and analysis, because you'll be asked to defend them. Use plans like this one for the process; make the design calls and write the analysis yourself.
